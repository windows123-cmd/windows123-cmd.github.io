The Challenge of Healthy Cities

Urban areas face critical problems that affect their inhabitants' quality of life. Spatial data helps identify solutions.

Data-Driven Diagnosis and Solutions

The NASA Space Apps "Data Pathways to Healthy Cities and Human Settlements" challenge invites urban planners, urbanists, and communities to use open data to address critical urban issues.

By analyzing data on air quality, surface temperature, vegetation cover, accessibility to services, and software such as QGis (threatened ecosystems, wetlands, protected areas), we can identify areas for improvement and design effective interventions.

Our platform facilitates access to this data and provides tools for analysis, helping you create more livable, equitable, and resilient cities.
